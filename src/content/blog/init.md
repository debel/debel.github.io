*I'll never be able to maintain a blog.*

I read all these amazing posts, and I'm always amazed at the ingenuity / creativity / curiosity of people.
The amount of stuff happening in the world is incomprehensible...

- An explosion of new programming languages - zig, odin, gleam
- A constant stream of papers on physics, chemistry, bio-engineering
- A deluge of advancements in every field of knowledge imaginable
- A barrage of technical curiosities - running a web server on a disposable vape??

**There are some truly amazing people doing awesome things!**

I am grateful that I'm able to learn anything I want, but then I think what have I *done*?

This blog is an attempt to keep myself honest, track my progress and share some fun stuff
of my own along the way.
